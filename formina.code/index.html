My Dearest, Tita pretty ♡

Hi ZA, Good day. How have you been? I hope you're doing the best and life has been treating you kindly and that you're well reading as of this moment. If you ever see this, ako nga pala yung nagka crush sayo nong first year. I made this small web sincerely to make up with you and longing to earn your heart again.

I'm truly sorry for the mistakes I've made, for my sleeping habits, my misunderstandings, my overthinking, and my immaturity that changed the way you felt about me. I'm clinging to the possibility that our story hasn't reached to it's final chapter. — Isa pa with feelings naman po.

Looking back, I realize that loving someone isn't just about having genuine feelings; it's also about understanding their pain, being mindful of my actions, and making them feel valued. I know I may be foolish sometimes, and I can't undo the hurt I've caused, but I don't want to keep making the same mistakes and giving you the same apologies. However, let me apologize for the last time (if not yet) na pinagsisihan ko na di ko nagawa yung promise ko before na pag napasaken ka ulet I'll give you everything that i am (ikaw kase kala ko meron ka ng iba, tapos sabi mo friends lang muna, ayun tuloy nakalimot ko).

Still, I want to learn, grow, and become better, not just through my words, but through my actions. I'm willing to try and try again to mend what's left of us rather than lose you entirely. No matter how difficult things become, a part of me will always believe that what we have is worth fighting for. Nonetheless, if you can no longer imagine being with me, I will respect your decision. I know we've lost some things along the way, but we've also gained something through everything we've experienced together. Regardless of this, can we atleast watch the movie that we said we would?

Truth to be told, I saw how hard you're trying for us, and I want you to know that every effort you've made and every sacrifice you've endured has never gone unnoticed. I see them, I acknowledge them, and I genuinely appreciate everything you've done for us and for the people around you. Especially the guilt you've had to carry while keeping our connection a secret from your father.

I like you more than words could ever explain, and the thought of losing you is something my heart still cannot accept. You make ordinary days feel special, and even the smallest moments with you become memories I want to keep forever.

Thank you for showing different kinds of you, for being yourself, for your silly smiles, I might as well mention someone who kurot kurot my tagiliran, your side na yearner, your pagka masunget, your dumpy side Mena, Ms. Pakipot na Mena and the one who sandal my shoulder. Lastly, thanks for the happiness you bring into my life. I may not always find the perfect words, but I hope with this I can show you how much you mean to me. I missed you pero ano basta lmk ur response nalang hays nakakahiya.

I want to keep liking you, just as I want to preserve this little page for eternity. And if in some distant place in the future, we see each other again in our new lives, I'll smile to you with joy and remember how we spent that autumn afternoon beneath the vines. I hope this reminds you that someone is always happy to have you in their life. You are my favorite person always, My Patchot, George, langlang, Mena'chanxutie, Mego.

Always yours,
Arese ♡
